Machine Learning in Football · 07

Can a hobbyist model beat the closing line?

Probably not — and that's the point. Every match at this World Cup, a Monte-Carlo model's probability is frozen before kickoff and scored against the bookmakers' de-vigged odds. The closing line is one of the most efficient forecasts in the world; losing to it honestly is worth more than a lucky win.

Paper evaluation only. Probabilities are shown for measurement, not as betting advice; no odds here are actionable.
